# ALIGNN formation-energy (eform) cross-dataset analysis

Aggregates all 20 eform arms (10 datasets x 2 pretrained model arms) into a
single cross-dataset / cross-functional rollup. Mirrors the structure of
`slakonet/slakonet_comprehensive_analysis/` and
`alignn/alignn_comprehensive_analysis/` (same `csv/summary_table.csv` +
`plots/` layout), but the target is **formation energy (eV/atom)**, not
bandgap.

The two model arms:

- **PBE arm** (`eform_v1_pbe`): `mp_e_form_alignn`, trained on Materials
  Project (PBE) formation energies.
- **OptB88vdW arm** (`eform_v2_opt`): `jv_formation_energy_peratom_alignn`,
  trained on JARVIS-DFT (OptB88vdW) formation energies.

## Scope

This layer is part of the **separate eform research thread**, not the SlakoNet
DB bandgap-prediction manuscript. Its outputs are not cited in that paper, not
in the croissant artifact, and not in the anonymous review mirror. It shares
repo infrastructure (input zips, conda env, cluster paths) with the bandgap
pipeline for convenience only.

## How the numbers are computed

The artifacts here (csv, plots, this writeup) are regenerated by a local
aggregator (`build_eform_analysis.py`, kept off-repo). It reads each arm's
authoritative per-arm aggregate:

- **Parity datasets** (a usable per-atom Alexandria PBE `e_form` reference
  exists): the `all`-subset row of each arm's `analysis/csv/metrics.csv`.
  These are `v03`, `v09`, `v10`, `v11`, `v12`.
- **Distribution-only datasets** (no usable per-atom DFT reference): each
  arm's `analysis/csv/distribution.csv` (prediction stats only). These are
  `v04`, `v05`, `v06`, `v07`, `v08`.

All errors are in **eV/atom** against the Alexandria PBE `e_form` reference.
The OptB88vdW arm's reference functional differs from the comparison reference
(PBE), so its non-zero mean signed error (`ME`) is the PBE-vs-OptB88vdW
functional shift, not pure model error.

## What stands out

**The PBE arm is label-matched and wins on hull-stable bulk crystals.** On
Alexandria PBE 3D, the PBE arm (`mp_e_form_alignn`) reaches MAE 0.029 eV/atom
on v03 (hull + Z<=65) and 0.036 eV/atom on v11 (hull, no Z filter), both near
the published validation accuracy of MP-trained ALIGNN e_form. The OptB88vdW
arm is 3-4x worse on these (0.117 / 0.102 eV/atom), almost entirely because of
the PBE-vs-OptB88vdW functional shift carried in its positive ME, not because
the model is less accurate.

**Low-dimensional geometry costs both arms.** v09 (1D) and v10 (2D) sit at
0.16-0.33 eV/atom, an order of magnitude above the bulk hull numbers, the same
geometry-OOD penalty seen on the bandgap side. v09's PBE arm carries a large
negative ME (-0.266 eV/atom): the MP-trained model systematically
underpredicts (more negative) formation energy for quasi-1D cells.

**v12 reverses the arm ranking at full off-hull scale.** On the full
4,489,295-entry Alexandria 3D set, the OptB88vdW arm (0.145 eV/atom) edges the
PBE arm (0.171 eV/atom). The full set is dominated by off-hull structures
where the MP-PBE model's bias grows (PBE arm ME -0.155 vs Opt arm -0.086).
This is a distribution-composition effect, consistent with the bandgap-side
v12 reversal, not a change in relative model quality on matched chemistries.

**Cross-arm offset is largest where geometry is most OOD.** The systematic
offset between the two arms (`plots/eform_cross_arm_shift.png`) is near zero on
slabs / vacancies / supercon (-0.002 to -0.030 eV/atom) but reaches -0.610
eV/atom on CCCBDB molecules and -0.356 eV/atom on 1D crystals, the two
geometry regimes farthest from either model's bulk-crystal training
distribution.

## Important caveat: v07 is not a parity dataset

JARVIS `vacancy_db` ships an `ef` field, but it is the **vacancy formation
energy in eV per defect** (the cost of removing one atom from a host crystal),
which is conceptually and dimensionally distinct from ALIGNN's **per-atom
formation energy in eV/atom**. The two are never parity-compared. v07 appears
here as a distribution-only row (mean predicted e_form per arm), and the
per-arm analyses only report cross-arm agreement, never a vs-`ef` parity.

v04 (molecules) and v08 (supercon) likewise have no per-atom DFT e_form
reference and are distribution-only. v05/v06 (slabs) could in principle carry a
JARVIS-propagated e_form, but the per-arm analyses conservatively treat them as
distribution-only rather than assert a parity against a possibly inconsistent
reference.
